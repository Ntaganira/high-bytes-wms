New reversal

Undoes a posted document whole: every movement it made is mirrored, the same item, place, bin, quantity and value, the other way. Nothing is keyed but the reason; the Internal Controller and the Managing Director judge the reversal by it.

Which posted document?

Goods received, opening balances, counts, write-offs and quarantine releases at this branch. Documents you raised or posted are not listed: whoever raised or posted a document takes no part in undoing it.
