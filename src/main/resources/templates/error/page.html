FORM EXPIRED

For your security, a form stops working when your session changes, for example after you signed in again in another tab. Nothing was saved. Go back, reload the page and try again.

ERROR 403

You don't have access to this page

Access follows your roles, and none of them grants this page at Gahanga. If you think one should, ask the system administrator.

A role granted at one branch gives its rights there and nowhere else.

The administrator account manages access only. Whoever grants rights must not hold operational ones, so screens that record or approve stock are closed to it by design.

Administration is divided too. Another administrator who holds this right can grant it to you: nobody grants themselves access.
