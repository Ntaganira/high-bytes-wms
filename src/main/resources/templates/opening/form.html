New opening balance

The stock a place already held when this system replaced QuickBooks. Saved as a draft, dated today (Kigali time) and bound to the approval chain in force today; the figures carry their own date, which is the day they were struck in the old books.

Cannot be loaded

reason

An opening balance is the first entry in the ledger at a branch, and a place takes one only once. Check the figures against the old books before submitting: every later figure is measured from these.

Who signs this

Four mandatory signatures: the Warehouse Manager who counted the floor, the Inventory Transactions Officer verifying it independently, the Internal Controller, and the Managing Director — because the figures become the opening position the Board is shown. Finance posts it.

Unlike a receipt there is no supplier, invoice or customs entry standing behind these numbers, which is why the chain is as long as it is.
