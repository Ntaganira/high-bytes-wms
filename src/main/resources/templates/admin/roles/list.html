Roles & Permissions

A role groups permissions. People are given roles; permissions are never given to a person directly.

New role
Role Structure Permissions Holders Chain steps Status
Finance Department FINANCE Both structures 17 3 4

Holders counts active accounts with the role in force today. Chain steps counts the approval steps the role signs in chains in force or scheduled; a role that signs any cannot be deactivated. A POLICY role is named by the segregation rules or an approval chain, so what it permits changes by migration only.

Segregation of duties

Pairs of roles one person may not hold at the same time. A grant that would break a BLOCK rule is refused.

RoleCannot be held withRuleWhySource
Internal Controller & Compliance Warehouse & Inventory Manager BLOCK Assurance cannot test work it performed itself. Board paper Table 6

The pairs follow the rights, not the names: a role created here that carries a right only one side of a pair has counts as that side. Separately, nobody may hold an administration right alongside anything operational (a transactional or master-data right, or an approval step), whatever the roles are called. Both rules are the system's, not rows here, so a role created tomorrow is covered by them too.
