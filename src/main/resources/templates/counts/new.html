Open a count

The system writes the sheet from the ledger and keeps the book quantities to itself: you count what is there, not what should be. From the moment the count opens, what it counts cannot move at the location until its verification is signed, so open it when you are ready to count.

What to count

error
Where stock is kept. Goods in transit are accounted for by their transfer, not counted.
error
Required when the location or the branch is bonded: a count may adjust duty-suspended goods.
Scope *
error
Every bin holding the item at the location goes on the sheet. Ignored for a full count.
